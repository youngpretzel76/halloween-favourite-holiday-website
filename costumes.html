costumes yay

costumes were always a difficult choice when i was younger cause what the hell was i trying to get at being tord for halloween im dead

i mean i had an end result whether it was something i liked or not. sometimes i'd have to change last second because the costume sucked and i didn't know how to go about it but whatever

most of the time though i was able to find a costume in time that i liked and actually worked out

if you're doing something last minute, don't bother hand crafting it. it's gonna take up too much time and before you know it, you'll have 4 hours before halloween actually hits and you're cooked

find an old costume that still fits or buy something from spirit halloween

speaking of which spirit halloween costumes are. something!...

do they work? oh yeah definitely. are they good? .....no

theyre cheap quality, theyre also extremely tacky

i would only really wear costumes from there if i were still a child or if they were cute accessories to a major part of my costume (also very rare occurrence)

but again if it was a last resort i'd still take what i can get. otherwise i'd have to pull up as stock image dude and i'd rather fight a gator in the nearby lake than do that..

also if you don't have expenses to make a costume then find something else. like if you cant buy materials but can afford a premade costume just go with that. do an inflatable or something.

if you're dedicated to making costumes, design and plan ahead WAY earlier. like start of october unless it's a short costume. then i'd still say like 2-3 weeks before.

moving on-costumes at school.

the school lets us wear costumes but with certain guidelines, obviously

we can't wear extreme face paint, we can't have fake blood, we can't have a mask covering our face, etc etc. all reasonable

i don't really remember a time where that was an issue but sometimes it's kind of annoying to think and remember that if you wanna wear your costume to school, you have to work around it.

for example, i went as sal fisher last year (i said this before) and the half of his face was blown off by a gunshot. of course it's gonna look gnarly as all hell.

i had to do a smaller version of it unfortunately. it worked! it was also fun to do at 5 in the morning then hop on the bus in my costume. but nonetheless my look later that night was so much better and sophisticated

again, i completely understand the regulations put in place especially with how schools are in america nowadays, but it still gets on people's nerves occasionally.

i won't really have an issue with it this year though.

all in all here's my advice:

DON'T MAKE A GIANT COSTUME IF YOU HAVE NO TIME, NO MONEY, AND NO MOTIVATION you literally will not complete it in time :')

second if you can't finish a costume just buy a premade one from a local store or something. there's plenty to choose from

be aware of if you wear it to school or in public (and it's not actually halloween time yet)

there's not much else to say just be safe, be diligent in costume making and have fun lol
